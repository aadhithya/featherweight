## PROPOSAL

### I. BACKGROUND

1. **Current Situation**
   - Fernhill tracks milk collection on paper route sheets.
   - Reconciling the sheets with invoices takes two staff three days a month.
   - Producers have asked for payment statements within five days of month end.

2. **Objective**
   - Replace the route sheets with a mobile collection app.
   - Produce producer statements automatically.

### II. SCOPE OF WORK

1. **Mobile Collection App**
   - Driver app for iOS and Android with offline capture.
   - Volume and quality readings recorded at each farm.

2. **Statement Engine**
   - Monthly producer statements generated as PDF.
   - Export to the existing accounting package.

3. **Data Migration**
   - Import of 18 months of route history.
   - Validation report for every imported producer.

## PROPOSAL

### I. BACKGROUND

1. **Current Situation**
   - Oakmere dispatchers plan routes in spreadsheets.
   - Last-minute changes are phoned through to drivers.

2. **Objective**
   - Plan and amend routes in one tool.
   - Push changes to drivers within one minute.

### II. SCOPE OF WORK

1. **Route Planner**
   - Planning view with drag and drop re-assignment.
   - Delivery windows and vehicle capacity respected.

2. **Driver Notifications**
   - Instant push of route changes to the driver app.

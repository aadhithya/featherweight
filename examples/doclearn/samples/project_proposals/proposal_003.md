## PROPOSAL

### I. BACKGROUND

1. **Current Situation**
   - Merriweather runs seven clinics that book appointments by phone.
   - Around 14% of appointments are missed without notice.

2. **Objective**
   - Let patients book and change appointments online.
   - Send reminders by text message.

### II. SCOPE OF WORK

1. **Online Booking**
   - Patient portal for booking, changing and cancelling.
   - Calendar sync for clinicians.

2. **Reminders**
   - Text message reminders 48 and 4 hours before an appointment.

3. **Reception Dashboard**
   - Live view of the day's appointments for each clinic.
   - Waiting list that offers freed slots automatically.

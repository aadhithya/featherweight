## PROPOSAL

### I. BACKGROUND

1. **Current Situation**
   - Larkspur sells through its web shop and 22 stores on separate stock systems.
   - Stock levels differ between channels by up to 9%.
   - Customers are sometimes sold items that are out of stock.

2. **Objective**
   - Give every channel one live view of stock.
   - Cut oversells to below 0.5% of orders.

3. **Timing**
   - The system must be live before the autumn season.

### II. SCOPE OF WORK

1. **Stock Hub**
   - Central stock service with an API for the web shop and the stores.
   - Reservation of items during checkout.

2. **Store Integration**
   - Connectors for the point-of-sale systems of all 22 stores.

3. **Reporting**
   - Daily variance report by channel.
   - Alerts when a product falls below its safety stock.

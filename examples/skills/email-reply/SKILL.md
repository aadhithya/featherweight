# Email reply

1. Decide the tone from who the sender is, then read `references/tone.md` and use its greeting and sign-off for that tone.
2. Write the reply in this order: the greeting, one sentence that acknowledges the message, the answer, the next step
   (with a date or time if one is known), the sign-off.
3. Keep it under 100 words, in plain language, with no filler such as "I hope this email finds you well".
4. Answer only what the message asks, and use only facts you were given. Do not invent dates, prices or promises.
